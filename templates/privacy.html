Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026 · Last updated: {{ privacy_updated }}

This Privacy Policy explains how Giro Gato ("Giro Gato", "we", "us") handles information when you play the Giro Gato mobile game (the "App") or visit girogato.com (the "Website"). For questions, contact {{ support_email }}.

1. Information we process

The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics. These services may process app-instance identifiers, device and operating system information, app interactions and events, session and performance information, crash logs, diagnostic information, and technical information associated with crashes. Depending on configuration and platform, Firebase may process other identifiers and metadata as described in Google's documentation.

You do not need to submit your name or email to browse the Website. If you contact support, we receive your email address, message, and any information you choose to include.

2. Why we use this information

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is used and improve the gameplay experience. We use Crashlytics to diagnose crashes, investigate technical issues, and improve reliability. We use support correspondence to respond to questions, investigate problems, and handle privacy requests.

3. Advertising and tracking

Giro Gato currently does not display advertisements and we do not use Firebase data to serve in-game advertisements. Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics nevertheless process certain identifiers and usage or diagnostic information. Whether any data qualifies as "tracking" under Apple's rules depends on the SDK configuration and how the information is used; our App Store privacy answers will reflect the actual released build.

4. Service providers and sharing

Google processes app analytics and crash data through Firebase on our behalf under its applicable terms. We do not sell players' personal information. We may also disclose information if legally required or reasonably necessary to protect the App, users, or our rights. Read Firebase Privacy and Security and Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Retention

Analytics and crash data are retained according to the settings and retention policies of the Firebase products we use. Support messages are kept only as long as reasonably needed to resolve requests, maintain records, and comply with legal obligations. Game progress stored on your device, if any, is controlled by the App and your device settings.

6. Your choices and privacy requests

You may request information about, access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we control, subject to applicable law. Email {{ support_email }}. Because Giro Gato does not identify every player by name, we may need additional details to locate relevant records, and some aggregated or anonymous records may not be attributable to you. Device settings may provide additional controls over app data and permissions.

7. Children's privacy

We do not intentionally ask children to submit personal information through the Website. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has shared personal information with us, contact {{ support_email }}. The game's intended age audience and any applicable child-directed safeguards should be reviewed before distribution.

8. International processing and security

Google and other infrastructure providers may process data outside your country. We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information, although no online system is completely secure.

9. Changes

We may revise this policy when our practices or services change. The updated version and its effective date will be posted on this page.

10. Contact

For support or privacy questions, contact {{ support_email }}. Website: girogato.com.
