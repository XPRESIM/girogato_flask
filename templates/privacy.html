This Privacy Policy describes how Giro Gato ("we," "our," or "us") handles information when you use the Giro Gato mobile game (the "App") or visit girogato.com (the "Website").

1. Information and technologies

Giro Gato uses Firebase, a Google service platform. Depending on which Firebase products are enabled in the released version, Firebase may process technical identifiers, app activity and usage events, device and operating system information, app performance data, and crash diagnostics. We must confirm the exact enabled products and settings before release; not every Firebase product necessarily collects the same data.

The Website does not require you to create an account or submit personal information to browse its pages. If you email support, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address and message.

2. How information is used

Information processed through enabled Firebase services may help us understand gameplay performance, detect and fix errors, improve stability, and measure app usage. Support messages are used to answer questions, troubleshoot issues, and respond to requests.

3. Firebase and service providers

Google Firebase may act as a service provider for the App. The exact Firebase services in use, their retention periods, and the categories of data they process depend on our configuration. For more information, see Firebase Privacy and Security and Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Sharing and disclosure

Information may be processed by service providers that operate the App or Website, such as Google Cloud and enabled Firebase products. We may disclose information when legally required or to protect our rights and users. This policy does not claim that the App is free of tracking or advertising-related data uses until the release configuration has been audited.

5. Storage and retention

Some game progress may be stored locally on your device, depending on the App's implementation. Support emails are retained as reasonably necessary to resolve requests and meet legal obligations. Firebase retention depends on the products and settings enabled in the released App.

6. Children's privacy

We take children's privacy seriously. The game's intended audience and age rating, and any child-directed restrictions, must be confirmed before release. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us at the email below.

7. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask about access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold, subject to applicable law. We may be unable to identify a particular player's anonymous Firebase records without an account or other identifier. You can also manage device-level permissions in your operating system settings.

8. International processing and security

Our service providers may process data in countries other than your own. We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the nature of information involved, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our App, services, or legal requirements. We will post updates here and revise the date above.
