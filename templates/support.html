How do I play Giro Gato?

Use the rotation controls to turn the puzzle, then plan your moves to align the numbered tiles and complete each challenge. Follow the instructions shown in the game for each level.

What if I get stuck?

Take another look at the tile arrangement and try a different rotation. If your version of the game provides restart or reset controls, use them to try again.

How do I report a bug?

Email us with your device model, operating system version, app version, a short description, and steps to reproduce the issue. Screenshots can help, but please avoid sharing sensitive personal information.

How do I request deletion of my data?

Contact us using the address below. Tell us which data you want deleted. Some device-local progress can be removed by clearing app data or uninstalling the game, depending on your device. Firebase-generated analytics or diagnostics data may not be linkable to a specific player.
