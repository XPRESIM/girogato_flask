✦ PLAYER SUPPORT

Need a helping paw?

Puzzle tips, troubleshooting, and a direct line to our team.

How do I play Giro Gato?

Rotate the puzzle and plan your moves to align the numbered tiles. Follow the instructions shown for each level; the goal and available moves can change as you progress.

I'm stuck on a puzzle. What can I try?

Look at the full tile arrangement before rotating again. Try a different sequence of rotations; if your version provides a restart control, use it to begin the level again.

The game crashes or won't open. What should I do?

Close and reopen the game, restart your device, and install the latest available version. If the issue continues, email us with your device model, operating-system version, app version, and what happened. Avoid uninstalling if you have progress that may be stored only on your device.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Email {{ support_email }} with steps to reproduce the problem. Screenshots are welcome, but do not include passwords or other sensitive information.

Does Giro Gato display ads?

No. The current version does not display advertisements.

What information does the game collect?

We use Firebase Analytics for usage statistics and Firebase Crashlytics for error diagnostics. Read our Privacy Policy for details.

How can I request deletion of my data?

Send a privacy request to {{ support_email }}. Include your device platform and enough context to explain your request, but do not send sensitive identifiers unless we specifically request them through a secure channel. Some anonymous or aggregated analytics records cannot be linked to an individual player.
